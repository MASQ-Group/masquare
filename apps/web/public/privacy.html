Privacy Policy

maSquare — internal operations platform of A.M.A. MASQUARE LTD. Last updated: 26 July 2026.

1. Who we are

maSquare ("the platform") is a private, internal business application operated by A.M.A. MASQUARE LTD and its associated company to manage their own e-commerce operations. It is not a public service and is used only by authorised staff of the company. Contact: sales@masquare.eu.

2. What the platform does

The platform connects to the company's own marketplace selling accounts (such as Amazon, eBay and OnBuy) through those marketplaces' official APIs, using credentials the company itself authorises. It imports the company's own selling data so the company can run its accounting, analytics, pricing, inventory and procurement in one place.

3. What data we access and store

Through the connected accounts we access and store the company's own operational data, including: order references, products/SKUs, quantities, prices, marketplace fees, taxes and settlement figures, listing and inventory information, and connection credentials (stored encrypted).

We do not store marketplace buyers' personal details — names, addresses, emails or phone numbers are not retained by the platform. Order information is used only in aggregate for the company's own sales and financial reporting.

4. How we use the data

Data is used solely for the company's internal business operations — reporting, analytics, pricing, inventory and procurement. It is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share the data with third parties. It is processed only by A.M.A. MASQUARE LTD and the infrastructure providers that host the platform on the company's behalf (e.g. cloud hosting and storage), under appropriate confidentiality terms.

6. Marketplace account deletion

Where a marketplace (such as eBay) sends account-closure/deletion notifications, the platform receives and acknowledges them. Because the platform does not retain marketplace buyers' personal data, there is no such personal data to erase; any applicable data is handled in line with the marketplace's requirements.

7. Retention

Operational and financial records are retained for as long as needed for the company's business and legal/accounting obligations, then deleted or anonymised.

8. Revoking access

Access to a connected marketplace account can be revoked at any time from that marketplace's own "third-party application access" settings, and by removing the integration within the platform.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the current version.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: sales@masquare.eu.
