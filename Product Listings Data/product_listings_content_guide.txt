Product Listing Generation Content Guide
----------------------------------------

1. Product Title (Unformatted Text)
   - A descriptive SEO optimized product title

2. Ebay Title (Unformatted Text)
   - A title optimized to be used for listing the product on ebay

3. Short Description (Formatted HTML Code)
   - A short descriptive sentense for the product (maximum 80 characters)
   - The 3 product main features in bullets. Each feature must not exceed 75 characters

4. Long Description (Formatted HTML Code)
   - A detailed description of the product
   - Do not include any title
   - It must be structured in sections
   - Each section must have an H2 Title
   - The last section must always be "What's in the box"

5. Key Features (Unformatted Text)
   - 5 Main product key features. Each feature must not exist 35 characters

6. Product Specifications (Formatted HTML Code)
   - A structured and formatted table including the product's technical specifications
   - The specifications must be orgnized in section based on relevance i.e. Product, Physical, Power etc. 
   - Include all the technical specifications you believe are important for a potential customer to view.

7. Search Keywords (Unformatted Text)
   - up to 10 most suitable, most searchable, best google ranked keywords, comma separated
